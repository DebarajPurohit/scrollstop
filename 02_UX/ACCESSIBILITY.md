# Accessibility

The app must be usable with Android accessibility features and should
follow Android accessibility guidance.

Requirements: - Content descriptions where needed - Logical focus
order - Adequate touch targets - Readable contrast - Scalable text - No
information conveyed by color alone - Blocking and permission states
must be understandable with assistive technologies

Note: This document describes accessibility of the app UI. It does not
imply that the product qualifies as an Android accessibility tool.
