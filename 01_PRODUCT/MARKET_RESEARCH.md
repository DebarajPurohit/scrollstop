# Market Research

## Purpose

Validate demand for stronger anti-doom-scrolling enforcement and
willingness to pay.

## Research Questions

1.  Which Android users repeatedly exceed intended social/video limits?
2.  Which existing solutions do they use?
3.  Why do existing timers fail for them?
4.  What level of enforcement do users actually want?
5.  What permissions create trust concerns?
6.  What price points are acceptable?
7.  Which features drive retention?

## Evidence Rules

-   Record source and date.
-   Separate observed facts from interpretation.
-   Do not treat anecdotal feedback as market-wide evidence.
-   Revalidate changing competitor and Play policy information.
