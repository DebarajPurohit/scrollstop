# Competitor Analysis

## Purpose

Compare competing digital-wellbeing, app-blocking and focus products
without copying them.

## Comparison Dimensions

-   App selection
-   Daily limits
-   Blocking enforcement
-   Override friction
-   Scheduling
-   History
-   Commitment features
-   Permissions
-   Privacy model
-   Pricing
-   Reviews/common complaints
-   Device compatibility

## Research Rules

Record: - Product - Version/date checked - Source - Documented feature -
User-facing limitation - Evidence

Do not make unsupported claims about competitor reliability or privacy.
